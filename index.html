Trust, not just stars

How the reliability score works

01 — On-time rate

Did the order arrive when promised?

Tracked automatically from booking confirmations vs. actual completion time. Weighted heaviest, since it's what students feel most.

02 — Cancellation rate

How often does the vendor bail?

Confirmed orders the vendor cancelled after acceptance. A high rate drags the score down even if reviews are glowing.

03 — Response time

How fast do they reply to a booking?

Average minutes between a student's request and vendor confirmation. Fast responders score higher on this factor.
