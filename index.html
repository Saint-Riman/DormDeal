How the reliability score works

01 — On-time rate

Did the order arrive when promised?

Tracked automatically from booking confirmations vs. actual completion time. Weighted heaviest for student peace of mind.

02 — Cancellation rate

How often does the Seller bail?

Tracks orders cancelled by the Seller after acceptance. High cancellation rates significantly drop overall scores.

03 — Response time

How fast do they reply to a booking?

Average minutes between a Buyer's request and Seller confirmation. Fast responders score higher on this factor.
